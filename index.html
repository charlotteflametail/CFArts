The Artist

Where myth keeps its shape

Charlotte Flametail is a fantasy-realism painter working in oil, ink, and digital media. Her work sits at the threshold between the world we know and the one waiting just past the tree line — drawn from folklore, fiction, and the private mythologies of the books she loves.

Each piece begins as a question: what does this character feel like to stand near? The answer comes slowly, in glazes and underpainting, until the figure feels less painted than remembered.

— C. Flametail
